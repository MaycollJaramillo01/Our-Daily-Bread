Our story

I have always had the conviction and the vision to start a business of my own.

Since I left Honduras I have had good mentors, and today I feel ready for this challenge: a project with a big vision for the future.

We make the most of basic, primary ingredients with a simple concept: high-quality food, always fresh that day.

And always helping the community, convinced that only the people save the people.
